# Project-1
This includes files for Project #1 for CIVE 202

The goal of the project is to analyze the air quality data collected by AirPurple across Nebraska from February 2024 to March 2025 for the UNMC Water, Climate and Health Working Group. The analysis will consist of evaluating concentrations of PM2.5, PM10, and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC) to identify if air quality meets NAAQS standards and determine potential locations of public health concern.
